The Firm

About MDR Law & Associates

A commitment to law. A commitment to society.

Founded in 2019 by Advocate R. Muralidharan, MDR Law & Associates began as a small chamber near the Madras High Court and has since grown into a full-service practice with offices in Parrys and Anna Nagar, Chennai. The firm advises on Civil & Property Law, Labour & Employment, Corporate & NCLT Litigation, Commercial Disputes, Contracts & Arbitration, Matrimonial & Family Law, Intellectual Property, and Media & Entertainment Law — including specialist advisory for YouTubers, digital creators and emerging filmmakers on content rights and brand collaborations.
